Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Who we are

lexic.on is a small web app for reading foreign-language texts and tracking vocabulary. Contact: pllattoff@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with GitHub or Google: your email address and your account ID at that provider.
  • The words you mark while learning, together with their learning status and target language.

The texts you paste into the app are processed on request and are not stored in our database.

How we use it

Only to sign you in and to provide your personal vocabulary. Individual words from your texts are sent to Azure Translator to get translations. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Where it is stored

The database is hosted on Neon, and the app runs on Render.

Cookies and browser storage

We only use technical cookies required for the login session and for CSRF protection.

Your browser also stores your chosen interface language (local storage) and the text you are currently working on (session storage, so it survives a page reload or the login redirect). This data stays in your browser and is not sent to our server.

Retention and your rights

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can ask us for access to your data, to correct it, or to delete your account and all associated data by emailing pllattoff@gmail.com. If you are in the EU, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

The services mentioned above (Neon, Render, Azure and the sign-in providers) may process data outside the EU.
